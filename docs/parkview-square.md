# Parkview Square (SG-235)

This model covers the 2002 Neo-Art-Deco office tower at 600 North Bridge Road — the "Gotham" building by Bugis MRT — with its statue plaza and golden crane.

## References

- [Parkview Square — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkview_Square) records a 144 m roof, 24 floors (2 basements), completion in 2002, American architect James Adams with DP Architects as local architect, the Chanin Building as its Art Deco inspiration, brown granite/bronze/lacquer/glass cladding, a 15 m lobby ceiling, the Piazza-San-Marco-like open plaza ringed with bronze effigies, the golden crane at its centre, and the eight giant crown statues — four on each broad side — holding light balls. The local OSM extract independently tags the parts 6 + 2 + 16 levels (24 in total), `start_date=2002`, and stone colours matching the dark plinth and brown shaft.
- Reference photographs viewed 4 November 2026 on Wikimedia Commons: [Parkview Square, Dec 05](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Parkview_Square,_Dec_05.JPG) and [Parkview Square y hotel Andaz, 2023-08-17](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Parkview_Square_y_hotel_Andaz,_Singapur,_2023-08-17,_DD_13.jpg) (a broad face nearly square-on: the charcoal granite plinth with its bronze circle-grille windows; the dark colonnade band across the middle at levels 6–8; above it a warm tan granite shaft cut by narrow dark bronze-glass slots, not a glass curtain; in the middle of the face a recessed field of six tall dark glass strips between narrow tan piers; either side of it a tan pylon standing proud, then a dark glass slot and a corner tier that steps back under dark cornices with lamps), [Top of the building Parkview Square in Singapore](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Top_of_the_building_Parkview_Square_in_Singapore.jpg) (the crown: the pierced bronze grille spans only the centre bay, in three panels between four piers, a statue holding a light globe on each pier, a stepped tan fin with dark caps rising over each pier; the tan pylons rise to the grille's top beside it, the corner tiers step lower, and the pale green glass lantern shows above both ends), and [Parkview Square from Arab Street](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Parkview_Square_from_Arab_Street.jpg) (the north-west broad face repeats the grille, statues and fins; the end face is tan with slots and a wider dark glass strip up its middle).
- [Esri World Imagery](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=10df2279f9684e4a9f6a7f08febac2a9) (z19 tiles, viewed 4 November 2026): the long glazed roof lantern runs most of the tower's length with a row of fins along each long side.

## Footprint and massing

The 25 October 2026 local OSM extract maps the site as the named outline [way 560274700](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274700) plus six `building:part` ways: the 1-level plaza slab [560274694](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274694) north-west of the tower, the 6-level tower base [560274699](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274699), the level 6–8 band split into dark centre [560274698](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274698) and brown ends [560274696](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274696)/[560274697](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274697), and the 16-level shaft [560274695](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274695) from level 8. These seven ways are the replacements; there are no relations or inner rings. DUO, Raffles Hospital and Guoco Midtown around the site remain mapped.

The model uses a local drawing frame from the tower ring's south corner (103.8576055°E, 1.2997776°N): `a` metres north-east (true bearing 44°) along the long faces and `b` metres north-west (bearing 314°). The tower is a 61 × 26.7 m rectangle in that frame; the plaza slab fills `b 26.6–64.2` on its north-west side. Along each broad face, from the photographs: a corner tier `a 0–8.9`, a dark glass recess `8.9–11.1`, a tan pylon `11.1–18.9`, the centre bay `18.9–42.1` (23.2 m, three 7.7 m grille panels between the four statue piers at `a 18.9, 26.6, 34.4, 42.1`), and the same mirrored. The mapper's 40 m dark band centre matches the centre bay with its pylons and recesses; the brown ends are the corner tiers.

Heights are photographic estimates keyed to the mapped level bands and the documented 144 m total: plaza deck 0.9 m; charcoal plinth to 32 m (levels 0–6, tall lobby storeys); the dark colonnade band 32–42 m between the corner tiers, which turn tan from 32 m; tan granite with dark slots (no floor lines; the slots' spandrels are dark) on the corner tiers to 113 m, with cornices at 58 and 112.4 m, an inset top storey to 124 m and a cap to 125 m; the dark glass recesses to 125 m; the pylons, 0.3 m proud of the faces, to 132.5 m under a cornice to 133.7 m; the centre fields, 0.6 m back from the faces, to 115 m; the crown cornice 115–117 m; the bronze grille 117–132 m, inset 0.6 m, under its cornice, the 132.8 m roof deck; the eight statues (9.2 m, on plinths from 117 m, globes to 128.4 m) on the piers; four-stepped fins over each pier from the deck to 144 m, the documented height, each stepping back from the face; and an attic in the middle of each end (to the deck) carrying the green-glass lantern, its corners cut, from the deck to 138.8 m under a low glass ridge at 140.2 m. The end faces keep a dark glass strip up their middle. The golden crane stands stylised on its plinth near the plaza centre (a 30, b 45) with six small effigy stubs around the deck; the rest of the plaza stays open in geometry and collision.

A first version of this model (PR #29 as opened) read wrongly against the photographs: a blue-black glass curtain across each broad face framed by a tan grid of floor lines, a bronze band around the whole crown and three black slabs on one side of the roof. It was rebuilt from the photographs above during review on 4 November 2026.

## Verification

- Merged with current `main` (50 modelled landmarks in all), the city generator retained 71,784 trees and 15,828 generic buildings & parts, with 642 mapped ways left to the modelled landmarks; regenerating the geodata from the merged registry reproduces the committed files. For Parkview Square, the tests check the seven exact replacement IDs, the mapped 24-level shaft and 6-level base, the stacked plinth/colonnade/shaft/crown at independently located coordinates, the open crane plaza, clear air over North Bridge Road, collision through the shaft, crown screen and roof lantern, and the photographed crown: the grille only across the centre bays, the pylons rising to the grille's top above the lower corner tiers, a statue and a 144 m fin on each of the four piers on both broad faces with none between them, the recessed centre fields, and the lantern on the roof deck. That last test fails on the first version.
- The full Windows suite passes 635 of 636 checks; the one failure is the known tile-worker fixture (`the tile worker: reads the land use once…`), which fails identically on `main`.
- The preview uses 1,372 triangles, 96 parts and 13 material batches, reaches exactly 144 m, and has zero coplanar flicker pairs. Perspective renders from the photographs' bearings (south-east and north-west faces, both ends, the roof) were compared side by side with the photographs in the Tiny and daylight looks, and the loaded city was checked from the map camera in WebGPU (DUO, Raffles Hospital and Guoco Midtown remain in place, with no duplicate generic Parkview tower).
- [East daylight preview](images/parkview-square-overview.png) and [city view](images/parkview-square-city.jpg). With the repo served locally, use `/tools/landmark-preview.html?model=parkview-square&view=e&look=day` or `/?look=tiny&at=parkview-square&camera=map&dist=500&pitch=55&yaw=-100`.

Simplifications at miniature scale: the statues and golden crane are stylised solids rather than sculpted figures; the crown grille is a bronze lattice material, its circular motifs not individually modelled; the rounded corners of the pylons, tiers and fins are square, and the fins' rounded "pillow" steps are boxes with dark caps; the cornices' bronze lamps are omitted; the lantern's curved glass corners are cut corners. Stage heights and the bay widths are estimates from the photographs against the documented 144 m total and the mapped 61 × 26.7 m plan.
