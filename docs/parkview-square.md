# Parkview Square (SG-235)

This model covers the 2002 Neo-Art-Deco office tower at 600 North Bridge Road — the "Gotham" building by Bugis MRT — with its statue plaza and golden crane.

## References

- [Parkview Square — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkview_Square) records a 144 m roof, 24 floors (2 basements), completion in 2002, American architect James Adams with DP Architects as local architect, the Chanin Building as its Art Deco inspiration, brown granite/bronze/lacquer/glass cladding, a 15 m lobby ceiling, the Piazza-San-Marco-like open plaza ringed with bronze effigies, the golden crane at its centre, and the eight giant crown statues — four on each broad side — holding light balls. The local OSM extract independently tags the parts 6 + 2 + 16 levels (24 in total), `start_date=2002`, and stone colours matching the dark plinth and brown shaft.
- Reference photographs viewed 4 November 2026 on Wikimedia Commons: [Parkview Square, Dec 05](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Parkview_Square,_Dec_05.JPG) and [Park view2-rect (undistort)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Park_view2-rect_(undistort).jpg) (the south-east long face and south-west end from the former open field: charcoal plinth with circular-grille windows, the dark colonnade band at its top, tan-and-dark vertically striped shaft with a darker glass centre field and curved corner bays, a mid-shaft string course, the crown's bronze grille screen, statues, stepped fins and pale-green roof lantern), [Top of the building Parkview Square in Singapore](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Top_of_the_building_Parkview_Square_in_Singapore.jpg) (crown detail: statues on plinths before the pierced bronze screen, stepped transverse fins, the green-glass lantern behind), and [Parkview Square from Arab Street](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Parkview_Square_from_Arab_Street.jpg) (the north-west face repeats the screen, statues and lantern).

## Footprint and massing

The 25 October 2026 local OSM extract maps the site as the named outline [way 560274700](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274700) plus six `building:part` ways: the 1-level plaza slab [560274694](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274694) north-west of the tower, the 6-level tower base [560274699](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274699), the level 6–8 band split into dark centre [560274698](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274698) and brown ends [560274696](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274696)/[560274697](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274697), and the 16-level shaft [560274695](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/560274695) from level 8. These seven ways are the replacements; there are no relations or inner rings. DUO, Raffles Hospital and Guoco Midtown around the site remain mapped.

The model uses a local drawing frame from the tower ring's south corner (103.8576055°E, 1.2997776°N): `a` metres north-east (true bearing 44°) along the long faces and `b` metres north-west (bearing 314°). The tower is a 61 × 26.7 m rectangle in that frame; the plaza slab fills `b 26.6–64.2` on its north-west side. Heights are photographic estimates keyed to the mapped level bands and the documented 144 m total: plaza deck 0.9 m; charcoal plinth to 32 m (levels 0–6, tall lobby storeys); colonnade band 32–42 m keeping the mapper's dark-centre/tan-ends split; striped shaft 42–112 m (16 floors of 4.4 m) with a dark string course at 84.5–86 m, dark-glass centre fields proud of the long and short faces, and dark corner bays; crown cornice 112–114 m; bronze grille screen, inset 1.6 m, 114–128 m with four plinth statues on each long face; stepped transverse fins to 141 m, the centre fin tipped at the documented 144 m; and the green-glass roof lantern behind them, capped at 141.5 m. The golden crane stands stylised on its plinth near the plaza centre (a 30, b 45) with six small effigy stubs around the deck; the rest of the plaza stays open in geometry and collision.
