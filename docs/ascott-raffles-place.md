# Ascott Raffles Place (SG-023)

The model represents the conserved former Asia Insurance Building at 2 Finlayson Green after the 2008 conversion to serviced residences. Ng Keng Siang's original building was completed in 1955.

## References

- [URA's conservation record](https://www.ura.gov.sg/conservation/find-a-building/conservation-portal/aib-00001/) identifies the travertine, the Nero Portoro marble of the double-height five-foot way and the stainless-steel coronation crown. Its Architectural Heritage Award gallery shows the street façades, gallery columns and an aerial view with the pool beside the corner tower.
- [URA's 2009 award submission](https://www.ura.gov.sg/services/download_file.aspx?f=%7B36994685-4904-4501-A05F-11C6E75A62F7%7D) and [2010 restoration account](https://www.ura.gov.sg/services/download_file.aspx?f=%7B867D6B4D-78CE-41C4-9DF5-EF474A467180%7D) document the conversion, new rooftop leisure area and retained original fabric.
- [Darren Soh's photographs for Docomomo Singapore, published 2021](https://docomomo-singapore.squarespace.com/modernist-100/asia-insurance-building) show the principal rounded corner, the two bands of projecting sunshades, curved window piers and fluted steel crown.
- [The Ascott operator's pool account](https://www.discoverasr.com/en/destinations/articles/elevated-city-stays-skyline-infinity-pools) confirms the rooftop pool. [Esri World Imagery](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=10df2279f9684e4a9f6a7f08febac2a9), viewed 4 November 2026, shows its orientation against the glazed rooftop addition. The imagery capture date is unspecified.

## Footprint and massing

The 25 October 2026 local OSM extract supplies the envelope [47345005](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/47345005), the lower wings [463857984](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/463857984), the setbacks [463857986](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/463857986), the glass addition [463857988](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/463857988) and the round tower [463857989](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/463857989). All five are replaced. There are no enclosing building relations or inner rings. No part of 6 Raffles Quay, One Finlayson Green or One Raffles Quay belongs to this site.

The mapped plan is approximately 38 by 27 m in axes at 121° and 211° clockwise from true north, from 103.8515646°E, 1.2822918°N. The ground glazing sits behind the line of columns. Overhead slabs cover the open five-foot way, which has clear spaces between the columns. The upper plan follows the two stepped mapped terrace profiles and the round tower. Heights are working estimates from the elevations and rooftop view. The upper terrace is about 68 m, the corner cylinder about 79 m and the three tiers reach the [reported 87 m height](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia_Insurance_Building). The OSM 25-level tag describes the envelope imprecisely. URA documents an original 18-storey office and a converted 20-storey residence. The city's address label follows the mapped alias, 2 Raffles Quay.
