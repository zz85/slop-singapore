# Oasia Hotel Downtown model (SG-115)

## Design and references

Replace the generic OpenStreetMap extrusion of WOHA's Oasia Hotel Downtown (100 Peck Seah Street, completed 2016; checklist task **SG-115**) with a procedural landmark model in the existing landmark kit: four full-height green corner cores, L-shaped floors beside tall sky-garden voids under a red aluminium mesh with creepers in vertical strands, and an open-topped crown ring curving inward over the rooftop terrace.

- [WOHA's project page (via world-architects)](https://www.world-architects.com/en/woha-singapore/project/oasia-hotel-downtown) is the architect's own record, quoted in search coverage: building height 190.15 m; four sky terraces at the 6th, 12th, 21st and 27th storeys; roof crown structure 42.9 m (about 13 hotel floors); 25,490 m² of expanded red aluminium mesh carrying 21 species of creepers. It describes the "club sandwich" of distinct strata — offices, hotel and club rooms — each with its own sky garden.
- [ArchDaily's project page](https://www.archdaily.com/800878/oasia-hotel-downtown-woha) (photographs by K. Kopter and Patrick Bingham-Hall, 2016) carries WOHA's drawings, which are the model's primary evidence: the 1:900 section (with scale bar; its width matches the mapped footprint, so its heights scale directly: terrace decks at about 26, 70, 114 and 144 m, the crown curving inward above the roof deck with no vault over it), the 6th/12th/21st/27th sky-terrace plans (service cores in all four rounded corners, each labelled "green facade"; the 27th-storey pools along the north and south faces around a central restaurant), and the typical-floor plans (7th–11th offices and 22nd–25th club rooms as an L on the east+north faces, 13th–20th guestrooms flipped to south+west, legs about 18 m deep, the opposite corner open as that stratum's sky garden). Its aerial photograph looks straight down through the open crown onto the rooftop pools. Area 19,416 m², year 2016.
- [Wikimedia Commons category: Oasia Hotel Downtown](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Oasia_Hotel_Downtown) supplied freely licensed straight elevations — "Facade A" and "Facade B" (2019) and the scale model exhibited at Roppongi Hills Mori Tower (2019) — for colours (vertical mesh panels in mixed red, orange, pink and near-white; creepers in vertical strands; corners almost solid green) and the signs' size and faces.
- [Wikipedia: Oasia Hotel Downtown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oasia_Hotel_Downtown) gives 27 floors and the CTBUH Best Tall Building Worldwide 2018 award; its top-floor figure (193.3 m) conflicts with the architect's 190.15 m total, so the architect's figure is used and the mapped 193 m tag is treated as an approximation.

## Mapped footprint and dimensions

- Replaced OSM object: way `393100847` only (`building=yes`, `building:levels=27`, `height=193`, `roof:shape=dome` — the last is the mapper's approximation of the inward-curving crown, not used). No `building:part` ways and no enclosing relation exist for this tower in `tools/data/singapore/osm_buildings.json`.
- The footprint is a convex rounded square of 20 mapped points after the city generator's simplification, about 50.6 m east–west by 49.2 m north–south (bounds centre 1.2758812, 103.8443413), area about 2,050 m² against a published site area of 2,311.4 m². Its corners are rounded with a radius of about 12 m, so each face is flat for only about 21.5 m (measured from the mapped ring's longest edges) — mesh panels, creeper strands and signs all stop at those flat stretches. In the model's local frame the footprint measures about 47.9 × 46.6 m between face planes.
- Neighbours are preserved: Guoco Tower's podium and parts (ways 393100845, 393100848, 393100849, 393100850) and the surrounding Tanjong Pagar buildings are not hidden.
- Model dimensions (metres, `y` up from the street): total 190 (documented 190.15). Terrace decks at 26, 70, 114 and 144, scaled from WOHA's section (the 144 m roof deck leaves a 46 m crown against the documented 42.9 m crown structure, a discrepancy inside the section-scaling tolerance of a few metres). Enclosed L legs are 18 m deep from their faces and run the full stratum between decks — offices 26–70, guestrooms 70–114, club rooms 114–144 — beside sky-garden voids of the same height (about 44, 44 and 30 m). Corner cores reach about 13 m along each face from their corners. The crown ring insets from the footprint by 0.25 m at 156 m, 0.9 m at 168 m, 2.2 m at 180 m and 3.5 m at the 190 m top, following the section's inward curve; its top stays open.

## Approach and scope

Build in a local east/south frame recovered from a mapped edge (independent of the vertex-average anchor and the city grid bearing), with the mapped ring itself clipping every footprint-following part:

- Four corner cores run from the ground to the roof terrace, each the footprint ring clipped to its corner (13 m along both faces), in a green strip facade with creeper strands sampled along the mapped perimeter at the corners — the full-height green columns of every photograph.
- Each stratum's enclosed floors are two legs of an L clipped from the footprint ring inset 1 m (the rooms behind the mesh), deck to deck, between the corner cores; offices and club rooms wrap the east+north corner, guestrooms the south+west, as the typical-floor plans show. The opposite corner stays open for the full stratum as its sky garden — a deck slab at its foot with pool or lawn, planting and trees clear of the water, and no filling slab in geometry or collision.
- The mesh is built as individual vertical panels in mixed red, orange, pink and near-white tones, only over each enclosed face's flat stretch as measured from the ring's longest edges, with creepers as vertical strands hanging on it. The base, below the first terrace, is a full-footprint block behind mesh on all four faces, with the Peck Seah lobby and canopy kept inside the south face's flat stretch.
- The crown is a ring of the same vertical panels following every mapped edge (rounded corners included, subdivided to about 2 m panels, each panel keeping its tone up the whole crown), lofted through insets that grow with height so the ring curves inward and stays open at the top; from above, the 27th-storey pools, restaurant pavilion, corner lifts and trees show inside it. Collision covers the corner cores, L legs, every terrace and roof deck, the restaurant pavilion and the crown ring in three bands at its own tapering insets, leaving the crown's middle and top genuinely open.
- Signs: white "oasia hotel" lettering hung with `kit.hang` on the club floors' enclosed east and north faces, sized to those walls so they sit on the flat stretches instead of floating off the curve.
- Interiors, the mesh's fine perforation, individual creeper species and terrace furniture are simplified to what reads at map camera distance. Reference photographs and drawings stay outside the repository; rendered comparisons are the model's own output.

## Verification

- New tests in `tests/landmark-models.test.cjs` probe the model from the mapped outline's centre in real east/north metres: exactly way 393100847 replaced (Guoco neighbours intact), 190 m top on the mapped footprint, no coplanar flicker, finite geometry, signs fitting their flat face stretches; four corner cores running the full height and solid at every corner mid-stratum, including the detached core in an open quadrant; each sky garden open from just above its deck to just below the next (30 and 66 m, 76 and 108 m, 118 and 140 m probes) beside its solid L; the crown open at its centre and top with nothing over the roof pools, while its tapering walls, every terrace deck, the Ls and the corner cores stop descent.
- Regenerated geodata on top of `main`, compared decoded against `main`'s geodata: `GEO.landmarks` gains exactly one entry (14 → 15 anchors, 241 → 242 mapped parts, the new part being way 393100847, anchor unchanged at the mapped site); decoded `GEO.buildings` go from 16,050 to 16,049 with exactly the generic Oasia extrusion removed (193 m, 27 levels, 20-point ring) and every other decoded building identical in absolute geometry (the only other packed difference is the next building's first-point delta, which is encoded relative to the previous ring). All other geodata keys — trees, lawns, blocks, roads — are identical, and `js/singapore-building-info.js` was regenerated in the same run.
- Targeted checks on the final merged branch: `node --test tests/landmark-kit.test.cjs tests/landmark-models.test.cjs tests/singapore-landmarks.test.mjs tests/city-geodata.test.mjs tests/building-info.test.mjs` passes 79/79. (Two flicker *timing* assertions in unrelated models intermittently exceeded their 2 s budget while other sessions loaded the machine during development; each passes in isolation in about 0.5 s and in the final full targeted run.)
- Rendered checks (headless Chrome, preview tool): [four-view Tiny look](images/oasia-hotel-downtown-overview.png), south elevation in day look and the north-east view on the WebGPU backend all render without errors; 9,182 triangles in 13 material batches. The rendered tower was compared against WOHA's section and plans, the ArchDaily aerial and the Commons elevations across two refinement rounds; the loaded-city headless capture hung in SwiftShader both times it was attempted and was stopped, so city context is verified by the decoded-geodata comparison and the preview renders rather than a committed city screenshot.
