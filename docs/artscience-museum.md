# ArtScience Museum model (SG-048)

## Design and references

Replace the generic OpenStreetMap extrusions of the ArtScience Museum (10 Bayfront Avenue, Marina Bay Sands; Safdie Architects / Moshe Safdie; opened 17 February 2011; checklist task **SG-048**) with a procedural landmark model in the existing landmark kit: the "welcoming hand" / lotus of ten white finger-like petals, of spheroid-generated form, radiating and rising in a height progression around a circular base, standing in its giant lily-pond reflecting pool on the Bayfront waterfront.

- [Marina Bay Sands owner factsheet (PDF)](https://www.marinabaysands.com/content/dam/singapore/marinabaysands/master/main/home/company-information/media-centre/factsheets/amenities/artscience-museum.pdf) — designed by Moshe Safdie; the building is composed of 10 "fingers", **the tallest standing at 60 metres above ground**. This is the model's documented height.
- [Wikipedia: ArtScience Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArtScience_Museum) — opened 17 February 2011; anchored by a round base in the middle, with ten extensions ("fingers") in the shape of a flower; each finger is a gallery space with **skylights at the fingertips**; rainwater is channelled through the **bowl-shaped roof into a reflecting pond** at the building's lowest level; 21 gallery spaces totalling about 5,000 m².
- [e-architect: Marina Bay Sands](https://www.e-architect.com/singapore/marina-bay-sands) (Safdie Architects' press text) — two principal parts: the base, embedded in the earth and surrounded by the bay's water and a **giant lily pond**, and a flower-like structure of **10 petals generated by the geometry of spheroids of varying radii** that seemingly floats above the pond base; the fingers progress in size and height around the centre.
- [Institution of Civil Engineers: ArtScience Museum](https://www.ice.org.uk/what-is-civil-engineering/what-do-civil-engineers-do/artscience-museum) — a circular core around which radiate 10 fingers housing different galleries; the tallest finger is 60 m above ground (search-indexed; the page itself returned a server error when fetched directly, so this is corroboration, not the primary citation).
- [OpenStreetMap relation 14314416](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/14314416) — the mapped building, its outline and its 60-plus `building:part` slices (see below).

Reference photographs (the Wikimedia Commons views from Merlion Park and the bay, and the aerial/roof views of the crown of petals) stay outside the repository; the model's whites, the petals' outward splay and the pond are taken from them as noted under "estimated" below.

## Mapped footprint and dimensions

- Replaced OSM objects (in `tools/data/singapore/osm_buildings.json`): **relation `14314416`** (`type=building`, `name=ArtScience Museum`; hidden via `hideRelations`) and **all 63 of its member ways** (hidden via `hide`, which also supplies `site.parts`): outline ways `261191086` and `827855854` (`building=roof`, `height=6`), the round base `827855915` (`height=13`), the lower bowl `827855929` (`height=14`), seven 1.2 × 1.1 m support columns `827855856`–`827855862` (`height=6`) ringed at 7.5–9.6 m from the centre, and the petal slices `827855863`–`827855930` (with gaps; `height` 12–26).
- Mapped dimensions (measured from the extract, about the round base's centroid, 1.286088, 103.859171): the outline way `261191086` covers 4,365 m² and reaches 36–54 m from the centre (longest to the south-east) — the pond/podium edge; the round base `827855915` has radius ≈ 11.0 m; the bowl part `827855929` reaches ≈ 21 m. The `building=roof` outline member `827855854` (centre 38 m north-east of the base) is not modelled as a separate roof — it is part of the mapper's slicing of the crown; hiding it removes its generic 6 m mass.

- **The mapped heights are not used as absolute heights.** They top out at 26 m, roughly half the owner's documented 60 m — the guide's warning that tags can describe something other than the whole building applies. They are used only as the *progression* (tallest to the north, decreasing clockwise, as the press text's "progress in height around the centre" describes): model fingertip = 60 × (mapped max ÷ 26), with the five western fingers — which the mapper flattened to a uniform 15 m — given a gentle continuing progression (38 → 32 m, the shortest beside the tallest, as a spiral progression implies). Those five heights, the fingertip reaches (≈ mapped reach less the tip slice's half-length), petal widths and all deck/pond levels are **visual estimates from the photographs and the mapped plan**, not surveyed values.
- Neighbours are preserved: the three low unnamed 3 m ways north of the pond (`172471575`, `172471576`, `620580651`), Da Paolo Gastronomia (`679034072`), the Shoppes podium slices to the south-east (`827847306` etc., SG-047's scope) and the Helix/promenade structures are not hidden.

## Approach and scope

The model's origin is recovered from the stable mapped round base (`827855915`, found by ID in `site.parts`), not from the generator's vertex-average anchor. True bearings come from `kit.compass`.

- **Pond deck**: the mapped outline `261191086` as a low stone-edged prism, with a dark lily-pond disc about the base and a scatter of small lily pads. The pond and promenade stay open — no solid taller than the deck stands on it between the petals.
- **Base and bowl**: the mapped round base as a white drum, under a flaring bowl loft out to the mapped bowl reach — the embedded base the petals spring from. The seven mapped 6 m columns fall inside the drum and are not drawn separately.
- **Petals**: each finger is three stacked lofts over ellipse rings along a spine that leaves the bowl, leans outward as it rises and tapers to the fingertip — spheroid fingers at landmark-kit fidelity. The fingertip cap is glass: the fingertip skylights. Petals are warm white (the FRP cladding in the photographs), the bowl a shade darker so the flower reads against the sky in both looks.
- **Entrance**: a low glazed entrance mass on the south-eastern face of the drum, toward the Shoppes link, estimated from photographs.
- **Collision**: the deck and drum collide automatically (ground parts); each elevated petal gets an explicit collision prism over its swept plan from the bowl to its fingertip, and the bowl a filled prism, so nothing can descend through the flower — while the pond between the petals and the air outside the outline stay open.
- No sign is modelled: the building carries no crown lettering in the photographs (its identity signage is at entrance scale). Interiors, the rainwater waterfall through the bowl's centre and lily planting beyond a suggestive scatter are simplified to what reads at map distance.
